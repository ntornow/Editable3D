# Modeling and surface completion tracker

Goal: complete reusable headless modeling and surface capabilities in Luau on top of EditableMesh/EditableImage. Version 0.7.0 was the starting point, not a completed parity claim. The required callable families are implemented through item 68, with documented contracts and bounded failure behavior. Release verification is recorded in `dist/verification.json`; function counts do not measure completeness.

The comparison baseline is the Blender 5.0 documentation already used by the package: [BMesh operations](https://docs.blender.org/api/5.0/bmesh.ops.html), [surface editing](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/5.0/modeling/surfaces/editing/surface.html), [surface primitives](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/5.0/modeling/surfaces/primitives.html), and [modeling modifiers](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/5.0/modeling/modifiers/index.html). The target is callable modeling behavior, not Python syntax, editor panels, keyboard shortcuts or selection UI. Required previously identified surface capabilities remain in scope even where they exceed Blender's native NURBS editing tools.

Rendering, simulation, animation, file-format expansion and application UI are outside this work. Procedural callbacks must be able to compose the modeling operations; a complete general Geometry Nodes catalog remains a separate objective.
